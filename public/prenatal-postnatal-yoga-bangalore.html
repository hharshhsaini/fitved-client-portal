Common physical adjustments we help manage

Pregnancy brings structural adjustments. We adapt exercises to support your body's shifts.

Back Pain & Alignment

As the belly grows, lower back arches increase. We stretch the hamstrings and build core stabilizers to alleviate nerve pressure.

Hip Opening & Pelvic Floor

We focus on dynamic movements to keep hip sockets loose and prepare the pelvis muscles for birth.

Breathing & Labor Prep

Breathing drills (Pranayama) to lower maternal anxiety, coordinate pelvic release, and control contractions.
