Session structure

A Typical 60-Minute Session

Every minute is intentional. Every session builds on the last.

01
0 – 10 min
Warm-up & Mobility

Joint prep, dynamic stretches, and breath awareness to wake the body up safely.

02
10 – 35 min
Strength or Yoga

Alternates by day — resistance training or a flowing yoga sequence, tailored to your level.

03
35 – 50 min
Core & Pilates

Deep core activation, postural work, and mindful movement to build real stability.

04
50 – 60 min

Static stretches, nervous system reset, and reflection on the session's progress.
