The 12-week fat loss progression

A realistic, structured timeline focused on building strength and dropping fat safely.

1

Weeks 1–4: Movement Foundation

We do a body composition assessment and check your joint mobility. Your coach builds safe movement habits, focusing on posture and lower back comfort. Our nutritionist designs a custom, Indian-diet-friendly food roadmap.

2

Weeks 5–8: Strength Acceleration

We increase session intensity using compound movements to boost your metabolic rate. You start feeling lighter, noticing your clothes fit better, and having higher energy during afternoon work hours.

3

Weeks 9–12: Consistency Integration

Refining exercise habits and locking in dietary changes. You can move comfortably without joint stiffness. We teach you how to manage portions during travel, festivals, and dinners so you maintain the loss easily.
